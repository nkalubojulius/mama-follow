Clinical Timing Tips

Why Spaced Meals Matter: Eating small, frequent meals every 2.5 to 3 hours prevents sudden drops in maternal blood sugar, alleviates nausea, and minimizes acid reflux.

  • Breakfast (8:00 AM): Eases morning sickness.
  • Snacks (11:00 AM & 5:00 PM): Maintains steady energy.
  • Dinner (8:00 PM): Keep it light and finish 2 hours before bed.
